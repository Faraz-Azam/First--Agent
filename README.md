# First--Agent
A Python AI agent that understands plain English — you just talk to it, no fixed commands needed. Built with Google Gemini.
What it does:

💬 Chat
Anything else — it just talks to you, powered by Gemini:
You: hello, who are you?
🤖 AI: Hi! I'm your assistant...


🌤️ Weather
Ask about any city, in any wording, and it pulls live weather data
You: what is 4 plus 6
✅ Result: 10.0

You: subtract 14 from 78
✅ Result: 64.0

You: multiply 5 and 3
✅ Result: 15.0

You: divide 20 by 4
✅ Result: 5.0


🌤️ Weather
Ask about any city, in any wording, and it pulls live weather data
You: what's the weather in Karachi
🌍 Weather in Karachi, Pakistan
🌡️ Temperature: 34°C
☁️ Condition: Clear sky
💧 Humidity: 45%

You: tell me weather in Tokyo
🌍 Weather in Tokyo, Japan
🌡️ Temperature: 22°C
☁️ Condition: Partly cloudy
